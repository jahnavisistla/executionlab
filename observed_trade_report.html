Research interpretation

The files support observed trade activity, turnover and price-path analysis. They do not supply contemporaneous bid/ask quotes, market-depth snapshots, order intentions or a client's executed fills. Therefore effective spread, order-book liquidity, actual client slippage and market impact cannot be identified from these files alone.
