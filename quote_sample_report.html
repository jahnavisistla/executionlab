Method

Replay visible add, modify, reduce, execute and delete events from the DataShop sample. Maintain outstanding order quantities and prices by order ID, reconstruct the best bid and best ask after book-changing messages, and calculate quoted spread in basis points as 10,000 × (ask−bid) / midpoint. Time-weighted mean spreads use elapsed time between successive valid top-of-book changes, bounded by the last observed update. Quotes with bid ≥ ask are excluded.

Interpretation

These are actual sample-derived quoted-spread calculations, not assumed spreads. They apply only to the sample symbols and date. The replay is deliberately a minimal implementation; it has not been certified against the exchange's reference book, and certain less-common message types are not fully modelled. Event median and time-weighted mean answer different questions; neither should be treated as a market-wide spread estimate.

Validation needed

  • Verify all message semantics, sequencing and session resets against the official PITCH specification.
  • Confirm order-book replay against an independent reference snapshot.
  • Inspect locked/crossed intervals, auction messages and potential incomplete initial state.
  • Obtain contemporaneous trades and quotes for the same symbols/dates to measure effective spreads.
  • Repeat across sufficiently many days, venues and instruments before inferring general patterns.

The raw Cboe sample and its event records are not redistributed in this repository. This report contains only high-level derived diagnostics.
