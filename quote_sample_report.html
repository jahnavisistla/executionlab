Method

Replay visible add, modify, reduce, execute and delete events from the DataShop sample. Maintain outstanding order quantities and prices by order ID, reconstruct the best bid and best ask after book-changing messages, and calculate quoted spread in basis points as 10,000 × (ask−bid) / midpoint. Time-weighted means use elapsed time until the next quote state change; missing or crossed books are excluded from the duration weights. The final quote is not extrapolated beyond the sample's last observed update.

Interpretation

These are sample-derived quoted-spread calculations, not assumed spreads. They apply only to the sample symbols and date. The replay has not been independently certified against the exchange's reference book. Extreme spreads and missing-book intervals are reported, not silently trimmed. Event median and time-weighted mean answer different questions; neither is a market-wide estimate.

Validation completed

Checked the numeric event codes against Cboe's official PITCH specification, including the explicit remaining-share field in execution-at-price messages. Added audits for duplicate IDs, sequence/timestamp reversals, over-reductions, and invalid quote-state transitions, plus targeted unit tests.

Validation still needed

  • Confirm the order-book replay against an independent authoritative reference snapshot.
  • Audit less-common auction and trading-status conditions and incomplete initial state.
  • Obtain contemporaneous trades and quotes for the same symbol/date to measure effective spreads.
  • Repeat across sufficiently many days, venues and instruments before inferring general patterns.

The raw Cboe sample and its event records are not redistributed in this repository. This report contains only high-level derived diagnostics.
