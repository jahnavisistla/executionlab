Assumptions and limitations

  • Each strategy has the same parent size, side and 15-minute observed trade-price path.
  • Execution capacity is capped at 8% of observed minute volume. Hypothetical fill price is first observed trade price plus a 1bp half-spread and nonlinear participation penalty; fees are 0.3bp.
  • The adaptive policy uses prior-minute volume against a fixed, ex-ante 1,000-share reference. It does not use future volume to schedule; the fixed reference is arbitrary and should ultimately be replaced with a calibrated historical profile.
  • Fill capacity is based on same-minute realised volume and is therefore an ex-post simulated constraint, not a live execution model.
  • Fill probability, queue position, displayed liquidity, auction conditions and venue fees are not calibrated.
  • Instrument selection is based on activity and minute coverage, which biases the sample toward liquid series.

What comes next

Add licensed quote history, apply strict quote-to-trade as-of joins, calibrate execution assumptions, extend to more days, and use instrument-day clustered uncertainty estimates.
