EXECUTIONLAB / CONTROLLED STRATEGY EXPERIMENT

Execution Strategy Comparison

Jahnavi Sistla · November 2026

Illustrative simulations on an observed trade-price path. The Cboe trades are real; parent orders, fills, execution capacity, half-spread and impact are simulated. This is not realised client TCA, a historical backtest with a full order book, or evidence of strategy superiority.

Experiment design

Instrument: FR0000120271 (EUR); period: 2 November 2026, 13:00–14:00 UTC; simulated BUY parent: 3,300 shares (1.17% of observed volume). Benchmark: observed trade VWAP 83.9276. Execution capacity capped at 8% of each minute's observed shares.

Strategies: immediate (seek completion earliest), TWAP-style (equal target quantity per minute), adaptive (target intensity based on the previous minute's volume). Simulated fills use the first observed trade price in a minute plus an assumed 1bp half-spread and a nonlinear participation-impact penalty; fees are 0.3bp of fill notional.

What can and cannot be concluded

This controlled comparison demonstrates order/fill accounting and how different schedules interact with one observed price/volume path. It does not infer the real fill probability or market impact of a client order. Results on one selected instrument and hour provide no basis for generalising to European equity execution. All strategies share assumptions, but their counterfactual executions cannot be validated without order-book and fill data.

Next research controls

  • Repeat across multiple dates, instruments, sides and order-size buckets.
  • As-of join real best bid/offer quotes and test timestamp sensitivity.
  • Calibrate cost and fill parameters, model queue effects and test fee schedules.
  • Apply bootstrap confidence intervals and walk-forward partitions by day.
  • Compare against a deliberately simple baseline, including incomplete orders.
