Methodology & limitations

A transparent audit trail is part of the product.

Data provenance

  • Cboe Europe MiFID delayed public trade records, 2 November 2026, 13:00–14:00 UTC.
  • The distributed source code excludes raw vendor market-data files.
  • Trade price and volume are observed; client orders, fills, spread assumptions and impact models are synthetic.

Known limitations

  • One trading hour cannot establish robust European market-microstructure patterns.
  • Trade prints do not supply the contemporaneous best bid/offer, full depth or queue conditions.
  • The strategy simulator is illustrative and lacks calibrated market-impact and fill-probability models.
  • No claims of live deployment, actual trading performance or strategy superiority are made.

Engineering controls

  • Explicit buy/sell cost signs and parent-order quantity validation.
  • Partial fills and marked residual opportunity cost.
  • Python unit tests and reproducible data transformations.
  • Next step: quoted and effective spreads from permitted time-aligned quote history; multi-day statistical validation.
