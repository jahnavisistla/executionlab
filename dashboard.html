How are these numbers calculated?

Signed shortfall currency = side × [filled shares × (fill price − arrival price) + unfilled shares × (end price − arrival price)] + fees. BUY = +1; SELL = −1.

Shortfall bps = total shortfall currency ÷ (parent quantity × arrival price) × 10,000. VWAP slippage bps = side × (average fill − market VWAP) ÷ market VWAP × 10,000, calculated only if shares executed.

Model specification · Evidence boundaries

Methodology & limitations

A compact audit trail for what was observed, what was assumed, and which conclusions the current data cannot support.

Data and pipeline

1. Historical trade observations

Public delayed Cboe Europe trade files from selected hours on 30 October and 2 November 2026. The published research processed 436,424 qualifying prints; no raw exchange files are redistributed through this dashboard.

2. Research scenarios

24 active instrument/venue/session series are converted into 336 hypothetical BUY/SELL parent-order scenarios. Observed minute trade-price and volume paths are reused; investor orders and fills are created by the model.

3. TCA engine

Measures signed arrival-price implementation shortfall, opportunity cost of residual shares, explicit fees, fill rate and slippage against a supplied VWAP benchmark.

4. Separate quote sample

The research also explores order-book reconstruction using a separate 2020 sample. Those quotes are not paired with 2026 trade observations. No combined empirical effective-spread estimate is claimed.

What the experiment assumes

Execution capacity

At most 8% of observed minute volume; this uses ex-post volume and is not a live queue model.

Fill prices

Observed first trade in the minute plus an assumed 1-bp half-spread and nonlinear participation-impact term; model fees are 0.3 bp.

Scheduling

Immediate attempts early execution; TWAP-style requests even slices; adaptive varies requests with previously observed volume and a fixed reference.

Statistical inference

Scenarios share instruments and sessions. Naive scenario-level resampling is descriptive and does not establish statistical significance. More dates and clustered/out-of-sample validation are needed.
