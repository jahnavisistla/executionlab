1. Methodology

Generate reproducible order records with buy/sell direction, arrival price, fill price, quantity, a stylised spread, volatility proxy, participation rate and venue type. All prices are arbitrary currency units. The simulation assumes an additive cost process: participation, spread, volatility and venue effects plus random noise. These assumptions deliberately create relationships for testing the analytics pipeline.

Implementation shortfall (bps): side_sign × (fill − arrival) / arrival × 10,000; +1 for buys, −1 for sells. Signed cost equals side_sign × (fill − arrival) × quantity. Positive values indicate adverse execution. This is a filled-order arrival-price shortfall measure, not a complete portfolio-level implementation shortfall calculation.

3. Execution and market-structure interpretation

In real European equities, execution quality depends on venue fragmentation, order type, lit versus dark liquidity, periodic auctions, spread, queue priority, market impact and the chosen benchmark. A credible TCA comparison must control for differences in order difficulty and market state; raw venue averages are especially prone to selection bias.

4. Limitations and next experiment

  • No observed market data or true venue-level liquidity; labels are simplified and are not claims about specific exchanges.
  • No partial fills, opportunity cost, bid/ask quotes, timestamps, fees, impact decay or dynamic order-book evolution.
  • Research extension: obtain licensed historical quotes/trades, calculate mid-price and effective spread, implement VWAP/time-weighted benchmarks, and compare execution cohorts using matching or regression controls.
  • Further extension: test outcomes of alternative participation schedules with walk-forward validation and realistic transaction fees.

5. Reproduction

Run python3 analysis.py using Python 3. The script generates synthetic_orders.csv and regenerates this report with a fixed random seed. No third-party packages or credentials required.
